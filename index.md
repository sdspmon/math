## Research Interests 

*Operads -- Category Theory -- $$E_n$$-Structures -- Foundations of Mathematics*

My research lies at the interface of category theory, logic, and algebraic topology. The purpose of [my thesis](https://theses.hal.science/tel-04617115) was to construct cofibrant models of $$E_n$$-operads governing the structure of $$n$$-fold iterated monoidal categories. My work on internal structures suggested an approach to the formalisation of mathematical language, structured around the hierarchy of $$n$$-categories and supporting iterated internalisations. I am working to make this language logically rigorous, with the long-term aim of developing a proof assistant.
